Questions

Is the event free?

Yes. The event and the Santa photos are both free. We'll email you a link to download your photos when they're ready.

Where are the Santa photos?

At River Rye Shoppe, the building right beside the clinic at 46 Shady Grove Road.

Do I need a photo reservation?

A reservation guarantees your time. Walk-ups are welcome too. We'll fit you in between scheduled photos, so there may be a short wait.

I have more than 2 pets. What should I do?

Each 5-minute spot fits up to 2 pets. Choose how many pets you're bringing on the sign-up form, and it will hold back-to-back spots for you.

Will there be food?

Yes. A food and beverage truck will be on site with items for purchase.
